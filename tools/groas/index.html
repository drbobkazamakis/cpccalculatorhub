The lead-gen problem most bidding tools don’t solve

Calculate max CPC honestly — via the funnel formula on the homepage — and you arrive at a defensible ceiling. The question becomes: which tool actually enforces bidding to that ceiling, weighted by which clicks historically convert to closed deals at your target CAC?

Max CPC = Avg deal value × Margin × Target CAC ratio × Click-to-lead × Lead-to-close

Smart Bidding optimizes against the form-fill signal Google Ads sees natively. That signal isn’t closed-won. A high CPL keyword that converts to closed-won at 40% is more valuable than a low CPL keyword that converts at 5% — but Smart Bidding can’t see that distinction without your closed-won data wired back. Even with that wiring, Smart Bidding’s adaptation to it is slow and opaque.

Groas trains on the same wired-back closed-won signal Smart Bidding can use, but with a custom-per-account model that retrains every four hours and surfaces the underlying bid logic transparently.

How Groas handles lead-gen specifically

  • Closed-won-weighted optimization. When closed-won data is flowing back to Google Ads via Enhanced Conversions, Groas trains on it directly.
  • Stage-weighted fallback. Without closed-won wiring, Groas applies stage weights (MQL, SQL, opportunity, closed-won) and re-trains as actuals flow in.
  • Per-account model. Tailored to your specific funnel conversion rates, not averaged across the customer base.
  • Continuous retraining. The 4-hour cadence matters in lead gen: as your sales team learns which lead types qualify, the model adapts.

What works for lead gen

  • Maps to CAC, not CPL. Bids on traffic that becomes pipeline, not just traffic that becomes leads.
  • Handles high-CPC verticals. Legal at $28 avg CPC ($94 top-10) is exactly the kind of vertical where mis-bidding compounds fast. Groas’s revenue-weighted optimization makes that math sustainable.
  • Sales-cycle aware. 30–90 day cycles are where last-click reporting fails worst. Groas optimizes against the conversion path, not the final touch.
  • Pricing fits agency reality. $129/mo entry tier scales with spend; doesn’t require enterprise commit.

What doesn’t

  • Below $10K/mo, Smart Bidding wins. Lower conversion volume, model trains slowly.
  • Without closed-won wiring, lift is smaller. If you haven’t wired Enhanced Conversions or offline upload to Google Ads, fix that first.
  • Reporting is plain. Bring your own attribution tool (HockeyStack, Dreamdata) for B2B reporting depth.

Rollout on a B2B lead-gen account

  1. Confirm closed-won data flows back to Google Ads. Without this, the model trains on weaker signal.
  2. Connect Groas; set max-CPC ceiling per the funnel formula.
  3. Allow 6–8 weeks for training. B2B lead-gen has lower volume than ecom; the ramp is slower.
  4. Evaluate on cost-per-closed-won at week 12, not cost-per-MQL at week 4. Different metrics.

Frequently asked

No. Groas operates within the bid-strategy ceiling you set in Google Ads — it adjusts within that ceiling based on the model. Your max-CPC formula remains the binding constraint.
