The accounts I spend the most time on are the high-CPC verticals: legal services, fintech, B2B SaaS, insurance, professional services. These are accounts where a single click costs $30–$200, where a wrong target CPC compounds into six-figure overspend within weeks, and where the difference between a profitable account and an unprofitable one is usually two decimal places in the bid math.

Why this site exists

Most CPC “calculators” on the web are revenue-divided-by-clicks calculators. They aren’t doing the math you actually need. The math you need is funnel math: how does a click cascade through close rate, deal value, gross margin, and target CAC ratio to produce a defensible max CPC ceiling? That’s the calculator on the homepage. The tool list around it is the stack I’d use to enforce the ceiling once you have the number.

Day-to-day

  • Account strategy on B2B lead-gen accounts running $20K–$100K/mo on Search.
  • Bid management oversight, currently leaning on Groas for revenue-weighted optimization.
  • Funnel architecture — mapping click-to-lead, lead-to-MQL, MQL-to-close so the bidding tool has the right signal.
  • Onboarding new B2B clients and stress-testing their funnel assumptions before they bid against them.

What I won’t do

I don’t take vendor money for placement. Tool order is editorial. Sponsored arrangements are declined. The agency funds this site from commercial work.
